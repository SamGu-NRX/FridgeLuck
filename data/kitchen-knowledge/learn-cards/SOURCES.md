## Nutrition data

- **USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy release (final release), downloaded 2026-10-11** — public domain (US government work). The zip (`FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_*.zip`) was fetched from the USDA download-datasets page and is not committed; nutrient IDs verified from its `nutrient.csv`. 108 of 116 cards carry nutrition blocks emitted by `tools/emit_nutrition.py` from the verified rows recorded in `tools/fdc_map.csv` (one-line rationale per choice).
- Eight cards ship without nutrition because no honest plain-ingredient SR Legacy row was matched (refused rather than guessed, listed in `index.json` `coverage.missingNutrition`): chaat masala, flattened rice, garam masala, gochujang, halloumi, harissa, paneer, rice vinegar.
