Cook with LeChef — the hob

Matte black, paper-white text, yolk for the two things that are live: the current step marker and running timers. No glass, no gradient, no transcript on screen. Cook mode ignores the system setting; it is its own palette, not "dark mode".

The step is the largest text in the app (26/32) because it is read from arm's length. Quantities and times inside the step are mono on a yolk-soft block so they can be found without reading the sentence.

Timers are tiles, not a list: 44 pt mono digits, a thin ring that drains linearly, tap to pause, long-press to remove. "Timer" adds one; by voice, "timer 3 minutes" adds one named after the current step.

LeChef speaks in one line, only when it has something step-relevant, with at most two answers. "Show it" opens the camera for a single look; the camera is never on by itself. A look describes what a picture can show and never gives a safety verdict.

Next is 64 pt and shows the next step's name so the user can decide without pressing. Back is a text link because it is rare.

States

voice off
"Just the steps" mode or mic denied: the bars become a static mic-slash glyph, hint reads "Tap to talk". Everything else identical. Timers and Next are unchanged.
offline
Live session drops: a one-line banner above the voice row, "LeChef is offline. Steps and timers still work." Voice falls back to on-device recognition for the five words. The LeChef block hides.
looking
"Show it" replaces the LeChef block with a 4:3 viewfinder for up to 3 s and the caption "Looking at the pan". Then the answer replaces it, limited to what a picture can show: "Browning looks even. I can't tell doneness from a photo, check 74 °C with a thermometer." Never a safety verdict.
timer done
The tile fills yolk, digits read 0:00 in ink, rigid haptic, sound. LeChef says what to do next in one line. The fill fades after the user taps the tile or says "next" or "repeat" (the vocabulary stays five words).
adapt
A quantity changed (one breast, not two): amounts rescale and the affected step shows a caption "Changed: 1 breast, same check: 74 °C" in yolk-ink. Times only change when a rule supports it (thickness, pan size), otherwise the step says to check rather than trust the timer. Never silent.
remember
At step 7, if the taste profile has a note for this recipe: "Last time you said too salty. I've cut the soy to 1½ tbsp." Shown once.
interrupted
App killed or phone locked: reopening returns to this step with timers still counting (they run on device, scheduled as notifications).
complete
After step 8: paper returns (320 ms), dish name, "Cooked in 27 min", kcal counting up once, a 1 to 5 rating row, "One line for next time" by voice or keyboard, optional photo, "Done". Nothing else. No confetti.
leave
The X asks once: "Leave this cook? Timers keep running." Leave / Stay.

Motion

next
Step text crossfades with a 6 px rise in 200 ms; the progress segment fills with the fade token. Said by voice ten times a cook, so never slower.
timer add
Tile enters from scale 0.96 with the move spring; the grid reflows with a transition, not a keyframe, so a second add mid-animation retargets.
ring
Linear. A ring that eases is lying about time.
voice bars
In the app they are driven by input amplitude. The mock's loop is a stand-in; a muted mic shows a static shape, never a fake pulse.
reduced
Step change is a 150 ms fade; ring still drains; bars still follow amplitude.
