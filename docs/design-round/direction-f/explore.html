Explore

Published community recipes and the catalog in one ranked list. The rank is the viewer's own fit line, computed on device from the Kitchen ledger, and it sits on every card: "9 of 10 · 1 swap", never a percentage. Search and filters work as everywhere else.

No like counts, no follower counts, no comments, no ranking by engagement. The response to a card is to cook it or save it. A recipe may say "cooked by 37" — distinct cooks, opted-in data, shown only at 10 or more; below that nothing shows, not a zero and not a dash (the third card has none).

Publishing comes from the personal cookbook as a separate previewed action; a published recipe is visible only to its author until the moderation check passes and can be withdrawn at any time.

Placement: reached from Cookbook (search and saved live there too), not a fifth tab. The tab bar shows Cookbook active.

States

empty
No results: "Nothing for 'vietnamese'. Try 'noodles', or clear the filters." Filters stay applied; the saved bookmark keeps its count.
kitchen unknown
No scan yet: cards replace the fit meter with "Scan first and this ranks by your fridge." The rank line hides. Nothing pretends to be cookable.
loading
The catalog is cached on device and renders instantly; remote updates arrive under one 2 px indeterminate line under the section title. No shimmer.
offline
"Rest of the catalog" hides; already-seen cards and your saved recipes render with fit computed on device.
moderation
A reported recipe hides pending review ("Hidden while we look"). A withdrawn recipe disappears everywhere except its author's cookbook.
counts
"Cooked by N" appears at 10 or more distinct cooks and only from opted-in structured outcomes — never notes, never photos, never inferred.
saved
The bookmark is a filter, not a social signal: saved recipes are private to the person who saved them.

Motion

enter
Cards rise 40 ms apart, capped at six; the rest appear.
filter
Chip fill crossfades 150 ms; the list re-ranks with a content crossfade under a stable frame (no reflow animation).
reduced
Fades only, 150 ms; no reordering animation.
