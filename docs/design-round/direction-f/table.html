Table — the feed

A table is 2 to 8 people who eat together or near each other. The unit is a cook, created by finishing one: dish, who, when, the one-line note, rating, substitutions, optional photo. Nobody writes a post.

Every card ends with the viewer's own fit meter and one button. That line is why this is not a dead feed: each card is a decision the viewer can act on with what is in their fridge. "Cook tonight" sets the dish on Tonight with Maya's note attached to the relevant step.

No likes, no comments, no follower counts, no ranking. Cards are in time order. Your own cook has no button because you cannot cook it at yourself; it is here so the table sees what you see.

The challenge block is setup live: the three ingredients are chosen when the owner creates the pack, joining costs nothing, and results come back as ordinary cook cards. Challenges work alone — you can send one as a link that reads without the app.

States

no table
Title "Table", then: "Tables are small on purpose: 2 to 8 people who cook. Start one with someone you eat with." Buttons: Start a table, Join with a code. Below, the notes section still renders from your own cooks.
alone
Table with one member (you): your cooks as cards, and a single row at the top, "Invite someone. Tables work from 2."
quiet
No cooks this week: the week line reads "Nobody's cooked this week. Your last was Sunday." Cards from last week follow.
invite
Share sheet with a code and a link: "Anyone with this link can ask to join Flat 4B. You approve each person." The owner approves or declines each request and can revoke the link or remove a member; anyone can leave, block and report. Former members lose access. The invite prompt never appears in onboarding; it first appears after the third cook.
challenge results
Entries render as cook cards in time order with a mono entry count. No voting, no leaderboard: the reward is that the table sees your cook.
loading
Cached cards render instantly; new ones arrive at the top with the rise animation. One 2 px line under the week row while fetching.
offline
Cached cards with "As of 18:40" in the sub line. Fit meters still compute locally. Challenge section shows with "Join" disabled and "You're in" if joined.
error
Fetch failed with nothing cached: "Couldn't reach your table. Your cooks are safe on this phone." Retry.

Table — share preview

Finishing a cook saves it locally. Sharing is a separate action, and the preview lists exactly what leaves the phone: recipe revision, servings, swaps, rating, an optional one-line note. The "never shared" line is part of the sheet, not fine print — the contract is the interface.

The same sheet is the publish preview for Explore, with one extra row: "I wrote this myself, or I have the right to share it." Publishing additionally requires the moderation check; until it passes, the recipe is visible only to its author.

States

nothing to share
The sheet only exists on a finished cook. There is no composer, no draft, no empty share sheet.
note by voice
LeChef transcribes the one-line note at cook-complete and reads it back here for confirmation before anything is shared.
aggregate
A separate opt-in below the sheet: "Add my rating and swaps to the recipe's totals." Structured fields only, never notes or photos, and separate from Table sharing.
account
Sharing requires a Sign in with Apple account and is adult-only in the first release (to confirm). Without it, cooks stay on the phone and everything else still works.
photo
No photo sharing in the first release. The card shows the dish name and your note, not your fridge.
