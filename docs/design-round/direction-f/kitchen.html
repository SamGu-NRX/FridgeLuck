Kitchen

The dense screen. 44 pt rows, hairlines, quantity and age in mono on the right. The eye should take in a shelf's worth without scrolling. No icons per ingredient: text is faster to scan than fifty tiny vegetables.

Order is by what needs a decision. Use soon first, each with the recipe that would use it (the warning is an action). Then "needs a look": items the app is unsure about, with the two answers inline so the truth model improves without a separate chore. Then the locations.

Row states by shape: filled square = confirmed, dashed = unconfirmed. Yolk tags carry the one meaning (attention now) and always sit next to a word.

Assumed staples are a sentence, not a list, because they are a policy rather than inventory. "Change" opens the checklist.

States

empty
Never scanned: title, then "Scan a shelf to start. One shelf, straight on, works best." with Scan groceries and Add by name as equal choices. No sample data.
stale
Last scan over 7 days ago: the updated line turns to a row, "Last scan 9 days ago. Tonight's picks may be off." with Scan now. Nothing is auto-expired; the app asks instead via "needs a look".
loading
None. Kitchen is local. After a scan, new rows enter at the top of their section with the rise animation and "today" in the age column.
error
None for reading. A failed sync to the backup is a single line at the bottom, "Not backed up since Tuesday", never a modal.
search
Inline field replaces the title; sections collapse to matching rows; "Add 'tahini'" appears as the last result when nothing matches.
edit
Tap a row: inline stepper for quantity and a date for use-by, plus Remove. Swipe left: Remove. Swipe right: Used up (deducts and teaches the app the real rate).
leftovers
Cook-complete can save a portion to the freezer or fridge as a row named after the dish with its date; it shows up in Tonight with its reheat time and a use-by date; frozen portions say how long they need to thaw.
receipts
Scans observe; purchases add. A shelf scan reconciles ("Still 2 lemons?"). Only a receipt or "New groceries" adds stock.

Motion

answer
Yes / Gone chip press: the row either loses its dashed state (150 ms crossfade) or slides out with the settle spring. Light haptic.
used up
Row slides right following the finger; release past 40% or a flick completes; the section count updates with the fade token.
reduced
Slides become fades; counts still update.
