Shell S — Progress

Progress, the second tab: three mono counters — cooked, streak, saved-from-waste — and the week's cooks in time order. F's structure (a tab, a dense list) with P's warmth kept out of the data: numbers stay ink, never accent-filled rings.

Saved-from-waste is counted in grams of food used before it spoiled — the app's ecological promise in one line. Every number here is computable from the cook log; nothing is a score.

Profile lives here, not on the bar: one row at the end, with the data controls (Forget) one tap away.

States

first run
“Nothing here yet. Cook one thing.” The counters render as 0 in ink-3, not dashes.
week boundary
Monday 00:00 local: the week list archives to Cookbook; streak survives if Sunday had a cook.
offline
Counters render from the local log (they are local); the cloud sync line shows “As of 18:40”.

Motion

count
Counters settle in place on entry; no count-up spin — the numbers are facts, not excitement.
