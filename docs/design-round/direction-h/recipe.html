A document, not a stack of cards: serif title, mono meta, photo, then the fit block as the one framed element because it is the app's promise. Everything else is hairline rows on the page.

Ingredients are quantity rows with the pantry-truth states: filled square = sufficient; hollow accent stroke = short or missing, carrying its swap inline in an accent-soft chip with the evidence (“you have 2”) and the consequence (“a little sharper, less floral” — never “no change”); grey ring = assumed staple, captioned; a dashed square with “check” = quantity unknown. Every substitution rechecks all eaters' allergens.

“Why it fits you” is three sentences with mono evidence counts; the heading links to the taste profile so every claim is inspectable and correctable.

Macros are three ink bars of different lengths with mono labels, not coloured rings. Cook notes appear only above the ten-cook minimum; this recipe has 212.

The floating dock holds the two entrances to cook mode: “Cook with LeChef” (voice on) and “Just the steps” (the default when voice is not opted in). It is the only glass element and the only shadow on a content screen.
