Scan review

The screen asks only the questions the camera could not answer, and asks them first. Three “needs you” rows, each with a thumbnail crop, one line about what it saw, and the two likely answers as chips (Miller's law: three uncertain items alone, then a list to skim).

The title count is mono because it is a number the user checks against the shelf. There is no confidence percentage anywhere — recognition confidence is not quantity confidence.

Selected chips use the accent the way F used yolk: one meaning (your selection), always with a word. Unanswered rows are allowed; the button still works and unanswered items go to Kitchen's “needs a look” queue rather than blocking.

The primary button names what happens and the count. The caption under it says why it matters. The dock is the one glass surface on a content screen (system glass), and the one shadow.

States

analysing
Photo at top, 4:5. Title reads “Looking”. One 2 px indeterminate line under it. Rows appear as recognised, each with the snap rise; no scanning laser, no shimmer.
all sure
The “needs you” section is absent. Title: “Found 9 things”, subtitle: “All look right.” Button unchanged.
nothing
“Nothing I recognise. Try one shelf, straight on, with the door light on.” Buttons: Retake, Add by name.
dark/blur
Detected before upload: “Too dark to read” or “Too blurry” with Retake. Shown over the photo, not as an alert.
error
Recognition request failed: photo stays, “Couldn't read this photo. Try again or add by name.” Retry keeps the photo; no re-capture required.
receipt
Same screen; a receipt adds stock. Rows carry the receipt line in caption under the normalised name (“ORG SPNCH 200G”). Quantities come from the receipt.
plate
Camera saw a cooked dish, not a shelf: a one-line switch at the top, “This looks like a meal. Log it instead?” with Yes / No. Never auto-switches.
duplicates
Item already in Kitchen: the scan observes, it never adds. The row reads “Still 2?” with Yes / Bought more — shown here on Lemons.
