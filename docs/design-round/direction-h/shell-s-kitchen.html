Shell S — two tabs + orb (ALTERNATE configuration)

Alternate, not the default. The default shell is four tabs — Tonight, Kitchen, Explore, Cookbook — with Scan as the separated trailing search-role tab (Tab('Scan', systemImage:'viewfinder', role:.search)) on the system Liquid Glass platter. This configuration keeps the custom central orb as the scan's home; it is the option that makes the scan a place, not a mode.

The orb is the single elevation: P's terracotta fill with the deep shadow, 56 pt, docked centre with its word. Everything else is hairlines. Progress is a tab here (F's structure) rather than a Kitchen sub-screen: cooked, streak, saved-from-waste counters in mono.

Hybrid spacing: the shell keeps 44 pt tab targets (Fitts) with P's 12 px label gap; the bar uses hairline top rule only, no material.

States

first run
Progress empty state: “Nothing here yet. Cook one thing.” The orb stays — it is the only permanently-visible control.
scanning
Orb pressed: expands to the camera sheet from its own position (origin-aware), dims the shell behind.
badge
Needs-a-look count lives in the Kitchen tab caption (“3”), mono, never a red dot on the orb.
