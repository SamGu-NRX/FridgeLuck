Explore

Explore answers “what could I cook that others made”, ranked by what you can make now: your own fit line, in mono, on every row. No like counts, no follower counts — “cooked by 37 people” appears only above ten distinct cooks and only from opted-in data.

Search and filters behave like everywhere else in the app. The fit meter's accent gap appears wherever a row is not 10 of 10, so the ranking reads before the words do.

User recipes and catalog recipes share the list without a visual caste. A LeChef draft keeps its own section, labelled “Made for your fridge”, and carries no cook notes until promoted by a tested cook.

Publishing is a previewed, separate action with a rights declaration; until the moderation check passes, a published recipe is visible only to its author.

States

empty
“Nothing published yet. Be the first — write one in your cookbook and preview before it goes up.” Buttons: Open cookbook, Search the catalog.
pending
Your published row shows a caption “In review — only you can see it” with a dashed square; it can be withdrawn at any time.
low counts
Any recipe under 10 cooks shows no count at all; the row reads “Be the first to cook this” and nothing is invented.
offline
Cached rows render with “As of 18:40”; fit meters still compute on device.
search
Zero hits: “Nothing matched 'tahini'. Fewer filters? Clear” — never a blank page.

Motion

rank
Refiltering re-ranks with the fade token (150 ms) under a stable frame; rows do not fly.
enter
First three rows rise on snap; the rest appear.
reduced
Ranking changes become instant fades.
