Table

Reached from Explore's “Community tables — Beta” row, not from a tab: the beta flag keeps the bar to four tabs plus the Scan search role. Everything below is unchanged by that move.

A table is 2 to 8 people who eat together or near each other. The unit is a cook, created by finishing one: dish, who, when, the one-line note, rating, substitutions, optional photo. Nobody writes a post.

Every card ends with the viewer's own fit meter and one button. That line is why this is not a dead feed: each card is a decision you can act on with what is in your fridge. “Cook tonight” sets the dish on Tonight with Maya's note attached to the relevant step.

No likes, no comments, no follower counts, no ranking; cards are in time order. Your own cook has no button — it is here so the table sees what you see.

The challenge block is the community candidate rendered quietly: entries reward cooks made and food saved, never opens. Share preview: sharing a cook shows exactly what leaves the phone — recipe revision, servings, swaps, rating, optional one-line note; never pantry, health data, taste data or exact times.
