Quests (candidate)

Quests, the community candidate, rendered in the hybrid's quiet register: no badges, no rarity colours, no progress rings with gradients. A quest is a sentence, a mono counter of cooks made, and “works alone” stated up front.

Rewards are use: finishing a quest adds a filter chip you've earned (“under 30 min”) and a line in Cookbook. Never points, never a currency.

The accent appears exactly three times on this screen: the joined state, the counter digits, and the primary button. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.

States

empty
“No quests this week. Cook something and it may become one.”
joined
Shown: joined state in accent-soft with the counter; Leave is quiet text, never destructive red — leaving is not an error.
done
The counter holds at its final value; the row reads “You made 4 — this one's done” with the reward chip attached.
offline
Last week's quests render cached with “As of Sunday”; joining queues and reconciles on reconnect with a visible receipt.

Motion

join
Joined fills on snap with a light haptic; the counter counts up once over 500 ms, mono, then holds.

Opens the share preview — the same disclosure as Table.
reduced
Count settles instantly.
