Your taste

The whole learning system in one readable page: sentences the app would say out loud, each with the count it is based on and a Right / Wrong control. No sliders, no percentages, no personality labels. Confidence lives in language and in counts.

Three layers keep their separation: explicit restrictions are the hard filters (edited in setup, never weakened here), stated preferences are the setup lines at the bottom, inferred taste is every sentence above. Evidence uses distinct opportunities with denominators and recency — “4 of the 5 times”, never “5 observations”.

The three row states: default Right / Wrong; confirmed, where Right holds in accent-soft and the row says what the app will now do (“Never suggested.”); rejected, where the row dims, the sentence strikes, and “Won't use this” sits beside Undo. Nothing is deleted on Wrong — it is excluded and kept so the app does not re-learn it next week. “Don't ask again” is a different control from “This is wrong”.

Hybrid note: the evidence counts and the confirmed chip are where the accent appears here (selection/attention); the sentences themselves stay in walnut ink — the learning page must feel like a ledger, not a horoscope.

States

new user
Under 3 cooks, one sentence instead of the list: “Cook three things and I'll start writing this. Until then I'm using what you told me at setup.”
rejected
Shown in the frame: the row dims, strikes, and reads “Won't use this.” with Undo. Undo restores it exactly.
forget one
“Forget this” under each remembered note removes that line with a visible receipt; the sentence can be reopened — changing your mind never requires a reset.
forget all
“Forget everything” at the bottom, in error red, asks once with the count of what will be deleted. Explicit restrictions survive any reset.
ask again
A weak inference expires on its own; the app never asks twice about the same pattern.
export
“Export everything I know about you” writes a readable file to Files. Learning runs on the phone; nothing about taste leaves the device except by that export.
