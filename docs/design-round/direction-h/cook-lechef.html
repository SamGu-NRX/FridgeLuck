Cook with LeChef

Cook mode is its own palette — the hybrid's “twilight hob”: P's warm charcoal (#1E1C17, the dark value of his deep-olive token, minus the olive) with cream ink and the light terracotta. It ignores the system setting, exactly as F specifies; the switch from paper to hob is the moment you commit.

Deciding density: 24 side, 32 between blocks. The step is 26/600, the largest body text in the app; quantities inside the step render in mono inside accent-soft. Targets are 64 pt because hands are wet (Fitts's law), including the segmented Next.

Timer tiles are the one card type: warm-charcoal surface, label in caption, digits mono 44–56 in the accent, and a thin ring that depletes linearly — a ring that eases is lying about time. Tap pauses; long-press removes.

LeChef's line sits under a hairline with its word mark in the section-label style — no sparkle glyph, no avatar. Its doneness copy is the checkable thermometer test (74 °C, USDA FSIS), never colour or time alone. A camera look describes browning and says what it cannot assess.

The voice indicator is driven by real amplitude; muted or offline it is a static shape, not a fake pulse. Voice is opt-in per cook: “Just the steps” is the default entrance from the recipe dock.

States

offline
One line under the header: “LeChef is offline. Steps and timers still work.” Voice falls back to on-device recognition for the five words; the bars go static.
timer done
Ring full, digits read 0:00, rigid haptic, tile lifts on the settle token; the step gains the check line “Rice done — stir the sauce in”. A timer that fires is information, so it still animates under reduced motion.
muted
Mic off: the indicator is five static stubs and the hint reads “Voice off. Touch controls below”. Never a fake pulse.
voice off
Entered with “Just the steps”: the voice row is replaced by a one-line “Voice off — Just the steps” with a quiet Turn on.
persisted
Kill the app at step 4; reopen to step 4 with the timer still counting. Step state and timer deadlines live on device.
adapt
Rescale at cook time: the step announces the changed step (“one breast, so 6 min not 8”) and timers follow the reviewed rule.
complete
The end of the cook is quiet: dish name in the serif, “Cooked” with the rating and one line, the 520 kcal line, Save. No confetti, no counting-up numbers except the single kcal settle.
