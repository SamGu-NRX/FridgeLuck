Shell S — two tabs + orb

F's shell verbatim, adapted: two main tabs — Kitchen and Progress — with the central scan orb between them, because the scan is the app's verb. Profile lives inside Progress; LeChef, Cookbook and Explore hang off Kitchen as header links, not tabs — two tabs plus an orb read as calm, five as clutter. Tonight remains the launch screen, one tap deeper than a tab but always first.

The orb is the single elevation: P's terracotta fill with the deep shadow, 56 pt, docked centre with its word. Everything else is hairlines. Progress is a tab here (F's structure) rather than a Kitchen sub-screen: cooked, streak, saved-from-waste counters in mono.

Hybrid spacing: the shell keeps 44 pt tab targets (Fitts) with P's 12 px label gap; the bar uses hairline top rule only, no material.
