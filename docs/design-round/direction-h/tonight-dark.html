Tonight

Deciding density: 24 px side margins, 32 between blocks, 48 above the primary action. One pick, its fit, its reason, and the button; two alternatives and the use-soon list come after, so the screen still works when the pick is wrong.

The hybrid keeps P's warmth (linen ground, walnut ink, terracotta as the single accent, a serif that appears only on the dish name) inside F's structure (flat content, hairlines, mono numerals, one accent with one meaning). The accent square beside “From your fridge” is the only chromatic mark above the fold; it marks the pick and nothing else.

The serif is the dish's voice: display size only, never on labels, controls or numerals. Every numeral with a unit is mono and tabular — both directions agree on that.

Fit is “9 of 10 · 1 swap”, never a percentage. In the meter the nine filled squares are walnut and the one missing line is the only terracotta pixel on screen: the gap is the story, and it always has a word beside it.

The Why line is evidence from the taste profile, written as counts, never flattery. Tapping it opens the profile sentence it came from.
