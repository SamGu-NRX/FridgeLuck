## Recommendation

**Take H, unless Sam's "ugly" verdict was really about the pastel look itself.** His working hypothesis was that the palette is right and the composition is wrong; H is the only direction that tests exactly that — it keeps his approved warmth and his resolved dark mode, adopts F's proven structure, and its weaknesses all have documented mitigations (surface rows under long lists, accent text riding accent-soft, accent digits only ≥24px). The cost, priced: body text loses its own voice (weight steps carry it), and sync/info states lose a second colour. That reverses to **F** if the round is really a vote for a clean break — F is the most coherent system and the most austere, and Sam should not pick it expecting warmth to be added later without redoing the neutrals. It reverses to **P** if "hammered down and I verify them" means his exact palette must survive for *text* too, not just fills — H and P share linen and dark mode, but only P keeps sage/oat/dusty-rose as live colours.

Whichever wins, nothing is lost: the other two directions' mocks stay on this branch as reference, Shell S and the candidates were rendered in every direction, and the design-system packages (PR #96) hold values behind a swappable theme — the round's choice drops in without touching components.

**After the pick:** Parts 2–7 (Tonight + Kitchen, scan review, recipe, cook mode, taste profile + Explore, Table + Profile) dispatch onto this direction with the identity components as their vocabulary; the `project.yml` hook and render workflow from PR #96 need Sam's approval as staged.
