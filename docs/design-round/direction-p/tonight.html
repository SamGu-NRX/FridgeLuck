Tonight

One pick, its fit, its reason, and the button — the 6:40 pm decision before the thumb moves. The hero is a single surface card on warm linen; everything else is smaller, denser and quieter so the pick owns the screen. Serif display carries the title, rounded carries the UI, tabular mono carries every number. Fit is “9 of 10 · 1 swap”, never a percentage.

Why is written as evidence with counts, never as flattery. The swap is named with its proof (“you have two”). A slim resume dock returns you to a live cook; it displaces nothing.

States

unknown kitchen
No scan yet or camera refused: hero becomes “Scan a shelf and I’ll pick tonight’s dinner.” with two first-class actions — Scan a shelf and Add food by hand (manual entry is never a dead end). A sample pick sits below, labelled “Example”.
sparse kitchen
3–5 items: the pick stays honest and the card adds a “You’d need” list with real amounts (“300 g chicken thighs, 1 lime”), then “Scan after shopping and this gets better.” No fake alternatives padded to fill.
nothing feasible
Best fit under 6 of 10: the pick is replaced by “Nothing cooks with what’s here.” and two routes — tonight’s shopping list, or a saved pick. The button never says “Cook with 2 swaps” when the fit is a fiction.
repeated rejection
Third tap on “Something else” opens a constraint sheet: Quicker · Different main · Less effort · Pick from a list. Rejections never become dislikes, there is no clock cutoff.
empty / loading
Local fit renders instantly; the Why and table lines arrive late into reserved height behind one 2 px indeterminate line. No shimmer.
error
Recommendation service down: last pick stays with “As of 6:10 pm” in the eyebrow chip. Use-soon, alternatives and fit are on-device and always render.
reduced motion
Entrance is a 150 ms fade with no stagger; press scale stays at 120 ms; the resume timer digits stay live (information, not decoration).
