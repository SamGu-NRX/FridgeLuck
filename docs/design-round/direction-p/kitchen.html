Kitchen

Answers “what do I actually have?”. Dense 44 pt rows with state squares — shape before colour: filled = have, hollow = missing, dashed = unconfirmed. A scan of one shelf refreshes only what it saw; every row carries its last confirmation and source (scanned time or receipt day), which is what makes “needs reconfirming” mean something.

Assumed staples are honest: they count as present for fit, say so, and can be unassumed.

States

empty
No scan, no entries: “Your kitchen is empty — scan a shelf or add food by hand.” Both actions first-class, equal size.
camera refused
The scan prompt never blocks the page; manual entry stays one tap away. Copy: “Camera off. Add by hand any time.”
stale evidence
A row whose last confirmation is older than the profile’s freshness rule moves itself into Needs a look with the age shown. It never silently keeps counting toward fit.
scan pending
While a shelf is processing, the affected section shows one 2 px indeterminate line; existing rows keep working.
quantity unknown
Recognition confidence is not quantity confidence: the row shows “?” in mono and stays unconfirmed until a real amount is entered.
reduced motion
Row press scale stays; section entries fade 150 ms without stagger.
