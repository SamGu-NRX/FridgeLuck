Cook mode — mid-cook (cook palette)

Cook mode has its own deep warm surface (deepOlive family), matte, no glass, and ignores the system appearance. The step is the largest body type in the app; amounts and durations are mono highlights; timers are first-class tiles whose thin ring depletes linearly — a ring that eases is lying about time. Tap pauses, long-press removes, and the timer runs on the device even when the session drops.

The offline banner is rendered live here on purpose: when the Live session drops, one line says so, voice falls back to on-device recognition for the five words, and the bars go static — no fake pulse when there is no amplitude.

Targets are 64 pt (Back / Next); every control on this screen clears 44 pt.

States

online
Banner absent; voice bars carry real input amplitude and the hint reads “Listening.” The camera look stays push-to-see with a visible viewfinder.
timer done
Tile settles once (480 ms) and the digits invert; haptic rigid. The step does not advance by itself.
adapted step
Adapt rescales amounts and flags the changed step (“one thigh, so 6 min not 8”); time changes only under a reviewed rule.
killed app
Step state persists: reopen at step 4 with the timer still counting from its persisted deadline.
reduced motion
Ring still moves (information), entrances become 150 ms fades, no tile bounce on done.
