Paper #F7F8F6 and ink #131313 for every deciding screen. Hairlines, no shadows inside content.
Hob #0C0C0C for cook mode only. Matte, no glass.
Yolk #F5C400 means attention now: the pick marker, a running timer, a use-soon tag, a selected chip. Nothing else.
Numbers are SF Mono, tabular, and the largest text on any screen where a number is what you read from arm's length.
Fit is squares and "9 of 10 · 1 swap", never a percentage.
Motion is five tokens: snap 220 ms, move 340 ms, settle 500 ms, fade 150 ms, none. Press scale 0.97 at 120 ms on everything pressable.
